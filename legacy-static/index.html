About Us

Rooted in Quality Since 2016

Established in 2016, MRN Agro Industries has grown into a trusted name in rice milling and processing in Karnataka. Operating from our state-of-the-art facility in Raichur, we combine traditional agricultural values with modern processing standards to deliver products that meet the highest expectations of quality and consistency.

From sourcing premium paddy directly from local farmers to ensuring hygienic processing and timely delivery, every step of our journey reflects our commitment to excellence.

  • Direct sourcing from farmers across Raichur district
  • Modern milling and processing facility
  • Installed milling capacity of 8 tons per hour
